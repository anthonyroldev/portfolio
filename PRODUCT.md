# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and engineering managers evaluating Anthony Rolland for a back-end engineering internship. They typically skim on desktop and have under a minute to assess his fit. The current search is for an international internship starting in May 2027.

## Product Purpose

Show Anthony's software engineering work and make it easy to contact him. A visitor should be able to understand his background, inspect a relevant project, and reach him directly.

## Positioning

Anthony studies computer science and cybersecurity at INSA Hauts-de-France and focuses on back-end software engineering. The project listing links to GitHub so engineering managers can inspect work such as his Java and Spring Boot APIs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English is the primary language for the international audience. The site also supports French.
- Visitors can browse projects and skills, open the CV, follow GitHub and LinkedIn links, or use the contact form.

## Brand Commitments

Use a sober, text-first presentation for Anthony Rolland's personal portfolio, with one blue accent. Reserve the accent gradient for the role and internship availability on Home. Keep surfaces flat and icons functional.

## Evidence on Hand

- `datas/projects.ts` holds project descriptions, technologies, and GitHub links, including RAG API and API PicIt. Project visuals are in `public/projects/`.
- `datas/skills.ts` lists skills, `public/cv.pdf` is the linked CV, and `public/me.jpg` contains Anthony's portrait.

## Product Principles

- Show what each project does and link to its source so visitors can judge the work for themselves.
- Make the most relevant back-end work easy to find and assess in a quick scan.
- Give visitors a direct way to get in touch after reviewing the work.
- Keep loading fast and reading, navigation, and contact accessible.
